              How the Oracle thinks

              The numbers are a forecast, not a guarantee. Football is noisy and a 60% chance still misses four times in ten.

              Team strength

              Every club gets an attack and defence rating from goals and expected goals in every finished gameweek, with recent weeks counting more and a pull toward the league average while samples are small.

              Fixture difficulty

              Expected goals for each side = league average × own attack × opponent's defence, plus about 12% for playing at home. A clean sheet chance is the chance the opponent scores zero.

              Minutes

              Recent minutes, season minutes and injury news set how likely a player is to start and reach 60 minutes. Doubtful players are scaled by FPL's chance-of-playing flag.

              Points

              Expected goals and assists per 90 are scaled by this week's attack outlook, then turned into FPL points with the official scoring, including clean sheets, saves, defensive contributions and bonus.

              Form

              About a quarter of each forecast comes from points per appearance over the last five gameweeks, adjusted for the fixture, so in-form players get a lift.

              Your chances

              Your eleven is simulated thousands of times with shared match results, so defenders from one club keep or lose clean sheets together. The average manager's score is based on this season's gameweek averages.
